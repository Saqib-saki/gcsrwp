💾 Weekly backup

Loading…

A full copy of every app spreadsheet (student lists and photos, attendance, marks, teachers and PINs, staff profiles, notices) goes into the folder GCS RWP Backups in your Google Drive. The newest 8 backups are kept; older ones go to the Drive bin, where Google keeps them for 30 days. Keep this folder private — don’t share it.

📊 Storage check

Google allows 10 million cells in each spreadsheet. Below 50% is fine. When a file passes 80%, close that class’s session (below) so it starts a fresh log.

📦 Close a session

Use this at the end of a session or BS semester. The class’s three spreadsheets are copied in full into GCS RWP Session Archives in your Drive. The app checks the copy, then empties that class’s attendance, lectures, totals and marks so it starts fresh.
Not touched: the student list and photos, teachers and PINs, timetable changes, staff profiles, notices, and every other class.

🔒 The college’s Google Sheets are private. Their links are shown only to admins signed in with an admin PIN, and only Google accounts the owner has shared a sheet with can open it.
